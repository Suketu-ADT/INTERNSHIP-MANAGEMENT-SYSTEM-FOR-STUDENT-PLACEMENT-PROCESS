Built for Both Sides of Placement

For Students

  • One place to search, filter and apply to internships
  • Instant eligibility checks before you apply
  • AI-assisted resume feedback and skill-gap guidance
  • Clear status tracking from application to offer

For Companies

  • Post internships with structured eligibility criteria
  • Review applicants and their submitted resumes in one view
  • Schedule and manage interviews with a full history
  • Make and track offers through to acceptance
